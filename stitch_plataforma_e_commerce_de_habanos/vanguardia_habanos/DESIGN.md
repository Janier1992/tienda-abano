## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the heritage, ritual, and bespoke sophistication of ultra-premium Cuban vitolas. Drawing heavily from luxury editorial publishing and tactile atelier craftsmanship, the interface evokes the tactile weight of cured Vuelta Abajo tobacco leaves, Spanish cedar humidors, and gilded lithographic cigar bands. 

The aesthetic is anchored in timeless luxury: disciplined whitespace, measured serif typography, understated gold accents, and a grounded parchment foundation. Interfaces must never feel sterile, overly technical, or utilitarian. Instead, they present cigar acquisitions, vitola archives, and sommelier reservations with reverence and gallery-grade composure. Legal compliance and age verification requirements are integrated seamlessly as marks of prestige, authenticity, and regulatory excellence rather than intrusive disclaimers.

## Colors

The palette derives from the natural materials of the cigar tradition:
- **Primary (`#1C1410` - Maduro Obsidian):** An ultra-dense tobacco black-brown that anchors hero typography, key interactive buttons, and primary structural panels.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` - Burnished Oro):** A warm metallic bronze-gold drawn from vintage Habanos seal foils and cigar bands. Used sparingly for accents, key borders, rating seals, and interactive highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#3E2723` - Spanish Cedar):** A deep, resonant wood tone applied to secondary containers, deep cards, and lounge-tier atmospheric surfaces.
- **Neutral (`#FDFBF7` - Alabaster Parchment):** The foundation canvas, providing an organic, non-glare, fine-paper tactile backdrop.

### Supporting Functional Tones
- **Typography Charcoal (`#212121`):** High-contrast body text ensuring effortless legibility without the harshness of pure black.
- **Subtle Rule & Boundary (`#E6DFD5`):** Muted parchment stone for dividers, grid borders, and hairline separators.
- **Regulatory Warning Surface (`#141414` background with `#FDFBF7` text):** Formally restrained high-contrast treatment for mandatory health warnings and statutory labels.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy strikes a deliberate balance between historical Havana opulence and modern horology-grade clarity:
- **Headlines & Display:** Playfair Display introduces literary grace, editorial weight, and heritage. Used for collection titles, vitola names, tasting note narratives, and lounge chapter headers.
- **Body & Specs:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides clean, contemporary legibility. It anchors provenance dimensions (cepo, ring gauge, millimetres), sommelier dialogues, transaction details, and statutory health text.
- **Letter Spacing:** All uppercase micro-copy (vitola specs, classification tags, badge indicators) utilizes deliberate letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to echo high-end embossed packaging and tax stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an architectural 12-column grid with generous margins to mirror collector folios and luxury broadsheets. 

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with `4rem` outer canvas margins and `2rem` gutters. Editorial blocks, product showcases, and tasting profiles use wide asymmetric arrangements (e.g., 5-column imagery paired with 7-column narrative specs).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content cascades gracefully with specs maintaining dual-column key-value pairings.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `1.25rem` outer canvas margin and `1rem` gutters. Spec matrices transition to stacked horizontally-scrollable badges or modular cards.
- **Rhythm:** Section-to-section spacing favors generous air (`space-2xl`), allowing featured releases and limited humidor offerings to breathe without visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through tactile tonal surfaces and low-contrast metallic definition rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Base Canvas:** Flat alabaster (`#FDFBF7`) creates an illuminated foundation.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** Layered using subtle tonal shift (pure white `#FFFFFF` or pale parchment `#F8F4EC`), framed by a 1px border of `#E6DFD5`. 
- **Active & Hover States:** Transition from standard stone borders to a precise 1px burnished gold rim (`#C5A059`) with an ambient gold-tinted glow (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.12)`).
- **Overlays & Drawers (Sommelier Concierge / Age Gate):** High z-index surfaces utilize rich Spanish Cedar and Maduro backdrop dimming (`#1C1410` at 75% opacity) accompanied by crisp hairline gold boundaries (`border: 1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined, nearly architectural precision (`Soft` / `0.25rem` base radius). 

Sharp, deliberate geometry reflects the precision cut of cedar cabinets, cigar presses, and guillotine cutters. Standard interactive elements—buttons, cards, inputs, and image frames—carry a minimal `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius. Badges and regulatory pills use a slightly softened boundary, avoiding bubbly or playful pill silhouettes. Pure square corners (`0px`) are reserved exclusively for mandatory legal warning banners and top-level navigation strips to emphasize structured formality.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Patrón):** Solid `#1C1410` background, `#FDFBF7` typography, 1px `#C5A059` border, `0.25rem` radius. On hover: background shifts to `#3E2723` with intensified gold border illumination.
- **Secondary (Oro Hairline):** Transparent background, 1px `#C5A059` border, `#1C1410` typography, uppercase `label-gold` styling. On hover: subtle `#C5A059` fill at 10% opacity.
- **Text Action:** Unbordered, `#C5A059` text with an animated 1px underline that expands from center on hover.

### Cigar Provenance Spec Badges
- Modular data tiles for *Vitola de Galera*, *Cepo* (Ring Gauge), *Longitud* (Length in mm/inches), and *Fortaleza* (Strength 1–5).
- Background: `#FFFFFF` encased in a hairline `#E6DFD5` border.
- The parameter name appears in `label-gold` uppercase; the value appears in bold `label-spec` `#1C1410`.
- Strength is rendered via five graduated tobacco leaf or seal pip icons filled in `#C5A059`.

### Product & Vitola Cards
- Cream/white surface with subtle inset margins mimicking classic boxed cigar bands.
- Enclosed with a 1px `#E6DFD5` border, upgrading to 1px `#C5A059` on hover.
- High-resolution, silhouette-cut imagery of the vitola against parchment, accompanied by factory vitola classification and year of harvest/banding.

### Age Verification Modal & Badges
- Full-screen modal utilizing `#1C1410` overlay with 85% opacity.
- Centered parchment card framed by a double hairline gold border.
- Clear, dignified legal gate: date-of-birth input selectors with elegant typography and an explicit statement of compliance with international tobacco statutes.

### Sommelier / Concierge Drawer
- Slides in from the right viewport edge (width: 420px desktop, 100% mobile).
- Background: Deep cedar `#1C1410` with `#FDFBF7` typography and muted `#C5A059` accents.
- Structured input bar with gilded send button and quick-action prompt chips (e.g., *"Pairing with Rum"*, *"Mild Morning Habano"*, *"Cabinet Aging Guidance"*).

### Regulatory Health Warning Banners
- Fixed, dignified black banner (`#141414`) with sharp `0px` radius.
- Stark, crisp typography in white uppercase (`regulatory-warning`), centered with generous vertical padding (`space-sm` to `space-md`), fulfilling legal requirements with authoritative elegance.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Crisp alabaster fill (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px border in `#E6DFD5`.
- Floating labels in `Plus Jakarta Sans` transitioning to `#C5A059` on focus.
- Focus outline: 1px continuous `#C5A059` stroke with zero offset blur.
